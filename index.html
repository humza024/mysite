ABOUT ME

Infrastructure is the product nobody sees — until it breaks.

I make sure it doesn't.

I'm a Senior DevOps Engineer with over 5 years of experience automating the path from code to production. My work sits at the intersection of development and operations: building CI/CD pipelines, containerizing applications, provisioning cloud infrastructure, and keeping a close eye on everything once it ships.

Across four teams — from startups to established agencies — I've deployed and maintained PHP, Laravel, WordPress, Node.js, NestJS and React applications on AWS and Ubuntu servers, backed by monitoring stacks like ELK, Zabbix and Grafana so issues get caught before users notice them.

I hold a Bachelor's in Computer Science from the University of Management & Technology, and I'm AWS Certified as both a Cloud Practitioner and Solutions Architect Associate.
